Next Up Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

Next Up is a to-do and notes app for Mac. It is built so that your information stays on your Mac and never reaches us.

The short version

  • Next Up does not collect, store or share any of your personal information.
  • Your tasks, notes and attachments are stored only on your Mac.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app makes no internet connections of its own.

What Next Up stores, and where

Next Up keeps everything you enter in its own private folder on your Mac:

  • Tasks and notes: titles, notes, tags, categories, urgency, links and dates.
  • Attachments: copies of files and screenshots you add to tasks or notes.
  • Preferences: settings such as light or dark mode and which groups you have collapsed.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive your data, and we cannot see it.

If you use Time Machine or another backup tool, your Next Up data may be included in your backups like any other file on your Mac. Those backups are under your control.

Internet access

Next Up does not connect to the internet. When you click a link you have saved in a task, it opens in your web browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies. When you open an attachment, it opens in the app your Mac uses for that file type.

Clipboard and files

Next Up reads your clipboard only when you paste into it. It reads a file only when you choose or drag that file into a task or note, and then stores a copy in its own folder.

Information Apple may share with us

If you have agreed to share crash reports and usage data with app developers in your Mac's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and statistics. These do not include your tasks or notes. You can turn this off in System Settings under Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements.

Next Up is free to try for 7 days, with a one-off purchase to unlock it. The trial and the purchase are handled entirely by Apple. The app asks the App Store on your Mac whether you have started the trial or bought the full version; we never receive your payment details or your Apple Account information.

Deleting your data

Delete any task, note or attachment in the app, and it is removed from your Mac.

To remove everything, quit Next Up, move the app to the Bin (called Trash in some regions), then delete this folder:

~/Library/Containers/com.alishasutcliffe.nextup

To find it, choose Go → Go to Folder in Finder and paste in that path.

Children

Next Up does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Next Up Support

Help with Next Up for Mac

Contact

Email alishafoxxyfox@gmail.com and I'll reply as soon as I can, usually within two working days. If something isn't working, say which version of macOS you're using and what you were doing when it happened.

The free trial and unlocking

  • Next Up is free to try for 7 days, with every feature.
  • After that, a one-off purchase of $9.99 unlocks it for good. There's no subscription.
  • If you don't buy, your tasks and notes stay on your Mac and you can still open, read and search them. Only adding and editing stop.
  • To buy, choose Next Up → Unlock Next Up… in the menu bar.

Restoring a purchase on another Mac

Choose Next Up → Unlock Next Up…, then Restore. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought Next Up with.

Adding tasks quickly

Click the checklist icon in the menu bar at the top of your screen. Type #tag to add a tag and !1, !2 or !3 to set the urgency, for example Call the bank #home !1.

Where your tasks are stored

Everything stays on your Mac, in Next Up's own folder. There's no account and nothing is sent anywhere. Time Machine backs it up along with your other files.

Moving to a new Mac

Migration Assistant brings Next Up's data across automatically. To move it by hand, quit Next Up on both Macs and copy this folder from the old Mac to the same place on the new one:

~/Library/Containers/com.alishasutcliffe.nextup

In Finder, choose Go → Go to Folder and paste in that path to find it.

Seeing the welcome screen again

Choose Help → Welcome to Next Up. From there you can add example tasks, and remove them later with the banner above your list.

Requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel Macs.
